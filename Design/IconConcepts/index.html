A new Sandboxes icon

Three takes on a child's sandbox with an agent's head poking out of the sand, on the plain macOS system background, next to today's icon. All are flat layers, ready to drop into Icon Composer. Glass highlights aren't drawn here; macOS adds those itself.

Appearance modes

Default, Dark and an approximation of Tinted. Tinted keeps only light and dark, so layers have to differ in value, not just colour. Clear behaves much like Tinted.

Recommendation

D2 or B. They're the two strongest. D2 scores best: its big blue box keeps today's colour and shape, so it stands out in the Dock even on the plain system background, and the graphite head still reads at 16 pt. B has more character, because the agent is bigger and peeking. Pick D2 if you want to keep continuity with the current icon, B if you want a mascot.

  • E is the warm version of D2. It scores the same as B and keeps the cheerful blue agent, but on a plain background it's less distinctive in the Dock than D2's blue box.
  • D needs the graphite head. With the blue head on blue walls, the agent disappears into the box at small sizes and in Tinted.
  • Background: a plain system background is allowed, but it puts B among Apple's white-tile apps. D2 gets away with it because the blue box does the job the sand background did.
  • A keeps the current isometric look, but the agent is too small to carry the icon.
  • C says “coding agent” most clearly, but the prompt glyph and spade are too much detail. If you like it, drop the spade and use C's dark head on B's layout.
